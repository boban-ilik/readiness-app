Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Readiness is built on a simple principle: your health data belongs to you. We collect only what is necessary to calculate your daily readiness score and personalise your coaching. We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers, and never use it for purposes beyond the app.

1. What We Collect

Health & fitness data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

  • Heart rate variability (HRV SDNN)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleep duration, stages, and efficiency
  • Step count and active energy burned
  • Exercise minutes and workouts

This data is read directly from Apple Health on your device. It is processed locally to produce your readiness score and is only uploaded to our servers if you are signed in and have an active session.

Strava data (optional)

  • Activity history (name, duration, type, date)
  • Training load metrics (suffer score, moving time)

Strava connection is optional. If connected, we fetch your recent activities via the Strava API to calculate training load and acute:chronic ratio. You can disconnect Strava at any time from your profile.

Menstrual cycle data (optional)

  • Period start dates you log, plus your cycle length and period length settings
  • The cycle phase and cycle day the app derives from them

Cycle tracking is off by default and only available if you turn it on in your profile. Your period dates and settings are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers. While tracking is on, the derived phase, cycle day and cycle length (never the dates themselves) are included in morning briefing and coach chat requests so the AI can interpret your metrics in context (see "AI-generated coaching" below). Turning tracking off stops that immediately.

Account data

  • Email address (for sign-in)
  • Display name (optional)
  • Profile preferences: age, biological sex, height, weight, training frequency, primary goal

Profile data is stored on our secure servers (Supabase) solely to personalise your readiness context and coaching recommendations.

Usage data

The app records a small number of product events against your account (for example: app opened, score shown, paywall shown, briefing opened, onboarding completed) so we can see which parts of Readiness are used and where people get stuck. These events carry no health values. They are stored with the rest of your account data on Supabase in the EU, are never shared with anyone, and are deleted with your account. The app contains no crash-reporting, analytics or advertising SDKs from third parties.

The AI features also keep a per-account count of how many briefings, coach replies and reports were generated, and when, so we can apply the fair-use limits described in Section 2.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculate your daily readiness score
  • Detect training load trends, overtraining signals, and recovery patterns
  • Personalise coaching recommendations and daily briefings
  • Send morning notification digests (only if you enable them)
  • Apply fair-use limits to the AI features (one free briefing a week on the free plan, and a daily cap on all plans) and confirm your subscription status with RevenueCat when an AI feature is used
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the Readiness app service.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

For readers in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom: the data controller is Boban Ilik, operating Readiness (contact details in Section 9). We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)) — for all health and fitness data. Health data is special-category data under the GDPR, and we process it only because you explicitly grant access: once through Apple's HealthKit permission screen, again for each optional connection such as Strava, and again when you switch on cycle tracking in your profile. You can withdraw consent at any time by revoking HealthKit access, disconnecting Strava, switching cycle tracking off, or deleting your account; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — for your account, syncing your scores between devices, and managing your subscription.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — for keeping the service secure, for the product-usage events and AI usage counts described in Section 1, and for enforcing fair-use limits on the AI features.

Readiness does not make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects. The AI features generate coaching text from your metrics; they do not decide anything about your access to the service.

4. Data Storage, Retention & International Transfers

Account and score data is stored on Supabase servers in the EU (Ireland). All data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Your readiness score is calculated on your device; the daily metrics behind it (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep duration and efficiency) are stored alongside the score so your history follows your account. That data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider and protected by row-level security, so it is only readable through your own signed-in account.

Retention. Account, profile, and score data is kept for as long as your account is active, and permanently removed from our servers within 30 days of account deletion. Coach chat history, cached briefings, and preferences are stored only on your device; they are wiped when you sign into a different account and removed when you delete the app.

International transfers. Some of our service providers process data in the United States: Anthropic (AI coaching text), RevenueCat (subscription management) and, for this website only, Google (analytics). Where personal data leaves the EEA or UK, the transfer takes place under each provider's data processing agreement, which incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple HealthKit — data read locally on your device, governed by Apple's HealthKit policies
  • Strava API — only if you connect; governed by Strava's Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — backend database and authentication; Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management; RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics — used on this website (thereadiness.app) only, not in the app, to understand visitor traffic; it sets cookies in your browser. Google Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude) — powers your daily insight, morning briefing, weekly report, and coach chat; Anthropic Privacy Policy

AI-generated coaching

Readiness uses Anthropic's Claude to turn your numbers into plain-English coaching. When a daily insight, morning briefing, weekly report, or coach chat reply is generated, we send Anthropic's API the data that reply needs:

  • Your readiness score and its Recovery, Sleep and Stress components, plus the metrics shown in your breakdown: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep duration, deep and REM sleep, sleep efficiency, daytime average heart rate and step count
  • Yesterday's workouts (type, duration, calories and estimated load) and any life events you have logged, such as alcohol, illness or travel, since those are what let the coaching explain a change rather than just report it
  • Patterns the app has detected over the previous weeks, in plain sentences (for example, a run of falling HRV readings or accumulated sleep debt)
  • Your cycle phase, cycle day and cycle length, only if you have switched cycle tracking on
  • Your first name, so the reply can address you directly, and a thumbs-down you gave the previous briefing
  • In Coach Chat only: the messages you send, and your profile details (age, biological sex, height, weight, training frequency and primary goal) so the advice fits you

We do not send your email address or any account identifier. Anthropic processes this data solely to generate the text you see, and not for advertising. Under Anthropic's API terms, data sent through the Claude platform is excluded from model training.

Your readiness score and its Recovery, Sleep, and Stress breakdown are calculated on your device from Apple Health data. Anthropic is involved only when an AI feature produces text for you.

6. Children's Privacy

Readiness is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR specifically gives you the right to: access your data; correct it; have it erased; restrict or object to its processing; receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time; and lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app controls below or email us; we respond within 30 days.

  • Delete account: Go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data from our servers within 30 days.
  • Export or access your data: Contact us at boban.ilik08@gmail.com
  • Disconnect Strava: Profile → Strava → Disconnect
  • Revoke HealthKit access: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Readiness
