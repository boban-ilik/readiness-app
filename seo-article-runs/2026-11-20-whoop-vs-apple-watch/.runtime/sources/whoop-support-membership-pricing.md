Membership Pricing

Nov 13, 2026

Overview

This guide provides information about membership pricing.

IN THIS ARTICLE: Memberships & Pricing; Membership Features & Benefits; For WHOOP 4.0 Members Who Do Not Upgrade; Upgrade & Membership Transitions; Downgrades & Membership Adjustments; Family Plans; Frequently Asked Questions

WHOOP offers three memberships—WHOOP One, WHOOP Peak, and WHOOP Life—each designed to fit different performance, health, and lifestyle needs. Membership includes access to the WHOOP app, continuous health tracking, and personalized coaching insights. Below is a breakdown of membership pricing and what’s included.

## Memberships & Pricing

Eligible new WHOOP Life members purchasing directly through WHOOP.com in the United States can select their preferred included band during checkout. Available band options are subject to stock availability, and the selected band will be included with their WHOOP device in place of the default Obsidian SuperKnit Luxe band.

Notes:

WHOOP One will be sold on WHOOP 5.0 hardware in the US, MX, CA, EU and UK. WHOOP One in these regions will be sold at $199/ year.

Outside of those regions, when purchasing from join.whoop.com WHOOP One memberships are provided with a certified pre-owned 4.0 sensor and Onyx Superknit Band.

New members can select a 24-month membership when signing up.

Members who sign up for an eligible 24-month WHOOP membership will automatically renew for another 24 months at the end of their current membership term, rather than renewing annually. Existing members on a 24-month membership will remain on their current annual renewal cycle, unless otherwise notified and changed.

Monthly billing is available to members who have completed their 12-month commitment. Monthly membership is not available to all members and may vary based on location. Please check your app or account page to view the plans available to you, which may include annual, buy now pay later, or monthly options.

Some features, like Healthspan, ECG, and IHRN require a certain device model in order to unlock. Additional accessories are available on Shop.whoop.com

Disclaimer:

ECG and Irregular Heart Rhythm Notifications (IHRN) are medically regulated features and may not be available in every region, please see Regional Feature Availability ( https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Regional-Feature-Availability ) for further reference.

If you are a current Month to Month member, you are welcome to stay on your monthly membership! If you wish to upgrade you can do so by paying a one-time upgrade fee.

## For WHOOP 4.0 Members Who Do Not Upgrade

Your membership will automatically transition to WHOOP Peak at your existing billing rate.

You can continue using your WHOOP 4.0 device without interruption.

You are not required to upgrade, and your experience remains unchanged unless you choose to transition to WHOOP 5.0 or WHOOP MG.

## Upgrade & Membership Transitions

Upgrading to WHOOP 5.0 or WHOOP MG (Medical Grade)

Current WHOOP 4.0 members can upgrade to WHOOP 5.0 or WHOOP MG by selecting one of the following options:

Extend Membership: Pay for the next 12 or 24 months upfront and receive new hardware free.

WHOOP Peak: $239 (includes 5.0 device)

WHOOP Life: $359 (Includes WHOOP MG device)

Pay Upgrade Fee: One-time payment to receive new hardware. Membership billing remains the same.

WHOOP Peak: $49

WHOOP Life: $79

Note: If you convert to a membership after your trial, you can stay on your same device or upgrade by following steps outlined in How to Upgrade Your WHOOP.

## Downgrades & Membership Adjustments

You may schedule a downgrade at any time in-app, and the change will take effect at your next billing date.

Note: you will not receive a refund when downgrading. You can continue using all the features of your original membership until the downgrade takes effect on your next billing date.

WHOOP One does not include Health Monitor, Stress Monitor, or Heart Screener features. If you downgrade from Peak or Life, those insights will become locked.

WHOOP One is designed for members who want to focus their insights on fitness, Sleep, and Recovery. Features like Stress Monitor and Health Monitor are included on WHOOP Peak and WHOOP Life memberships and if you downgrade from Peak or Life, those insights will become locked.

## Family Plans

Family memberships are available across all memberships and all members must be on the same membership.

The family admin may upgrade or downgrade the entire family membership.

Downgrades take effect at the next billing date, while upgrades are immediate.

## Additional Notes & Policies

Membership Renewals: WHOOP memberships automatically renew based on the applicable membership term unless canceled before the next renewal date. Annual memberships renew every 12 months, while eligible 24-month memberships automatically renew for another 24 months. Existing members on a 24-month membership will remain on their current annual renewal cycle, unless otherwise notified and changed.

For additional information about membership renewal cycles, see Billing Renewal Cycle & Timeline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade to WHOOP Life?

You can upgrade via the WHOOP app or Join Flow by selecting WHOOP Life and either extending your membership by 12 months or 24-months or paying a $79 upgrade fee.

Can I keep my WHOOP 4.0 if I upgrade?

Yes! If you upgrade to WHOOP 5.0 or WHOOP MG, you are encouraged to upcycle your 4.0 device by giving it to a friend or family member. If they become a member, you’ll receive a $50 WHOOP Shop credit*.

Note: upgrading members are given 2 options during checkout for how they’d like to upcycle their device. See How to Upcycle Your WHOOP 4.0 for more detail.

What happens if I don’t upgrade my WHOOP 4.0?

You’ll continue receiving the insights you know and love—your current membership will now be called WHOOP Peak, with no change to your billing or your WHOOP 4.0 features.

Will I need to return my WHOOP 4.0 if I upgrade?

No! Your WHOOP 4.0 is yours to keep or upcycle to a friend or family member.

Can I buy a large amount of 5.0 or MG devices?

Yes! You can purchase as many as you’d like: For orders of 50 or fewer, our Inside Sales team can assist you! For orders of 50 or more, our Partnerships team can help with your purchase!

Will WHOOP Life itself be age restricted, since most of its features are age restricted themselves?

No, WHOOP Life itself is not age-restricted—anyone can purchase the Medical Grade device. However, some features may have age requirements and won’t be available to all members.
