Apple Health Integration

Apr 16, 2026

Overview

WHOOP members using on iOS can connect to Apple Health to sync:

Recovery Metrics: Resting Heart Rate (RHR), Blood Oxygen (SpO2), and Respiratory Rate.

Strain & Workouts: Import activities from Apple Health and sync WHOOP heart rate data.

Sleep Tracking: WHOOP automatically logs sleep sessions in Apple Health.

Body Measurements: WHOOP syncs height and weight from Apple Health for improved calorie tracking.

How to Connect WHOOP to Apple Health

To enable Apple Health integration, follow these steps:

Ensure your WHOOP app is up to date by checking for the latest version in the App Store. To check: open the App Store app, type in WHOOP, tap on the page, and press “UPDATE” if it appears.

Open the WHOOP app, then navigate to the More menu.

Scroll down and select App Settings.

Tap Integrations.

Select Apple Health at the top of the page.

Tap Connect and follow the prompts.

Choose Turn on all or select specific data categories you want to sync.

Tap Allow to confirm the connection.

Note: WHOOP members can also enable Apple Health integration during the onboarding process when setting up their device.

Managing Shared Data with Apple Health

To control what data WHOOP shares with Apple Health:

Open the Health app on your iPhone.

Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.

Select Apps.

Choose WHOOP from the list.

Toggle on or off the data categories you want WHOOP to sync.

Frequently Asked Questions

Importing Data

How does WHOOP use Apple Health data?

WHOOP uses imported activity and biometric data to refine analytics and personalize your profile.

Why aren’t my Apple Health activities auto-creating workouts in WHOOP?

Ensure "read" permissions for WHOOP are enabled in Apple Health.

Some apps/devices require manual sync—try refreshing your WHOOP app.

Can I edit Apple Health activities once they are imported into WHOOP?

Yes, you can edit any imported activity for up to 30 days in the WHOOP app.

What happens if an imported Apple Health activity overlaps with an existing WHOOP auto-detected activity?

If an imported Apple Health activity overlaps with an existing WHOOP auto-detected activity, the imported activity will overwrite the auto-detected one, including the time bounds and activity type. If needed, you can easily edit the imported activity afterward to adjust those details.

Can I manually add activities in WHOOP?

Yes, you can manually log activities within the WHOOP app.

Exporting Data

Why do I see duplicate activities in Apple Health?

Duplicate activities can appear if multiple sources, such as WHOOP and another app or device, are writing workout data to Apple Health at the same time. If both import and export are enabled for WHOOP, this can increase the likelihood of duplicate entries. To help prevent this, you can disable "Workouts" export from WHOOP in your Apple Health settings.

What happens if I update or delete an activity in WHOOP after it’s exported to Apple Health?

WHOOP will update the corresponding entry in Apple Health.

Why is my HRV missing in Apple Health?

Apple Health uses SDNN (Standard Deviation of Normal-to-Normal) for HRV, while WHOOP calculates RMSSD (root mean square of successive differences).

WHOOP does not export HRV data to Apple Health at this time.

Can I disable WHOOP from writing workouts to Apple Health?

Yes, you can toggle off "Workouts" in Apple Health settings at any time.

Auto-Log Nutrition

What is Auto-Log Nutrition?

This feature allows third-party apps to automatically sync nutrition data into the WHOOP Journal.

How do I fix missing nutrition data?

Ensure that third-party apps have permission to write to Apple Health.

Reconnect apps if syncing issues persist.

What if I forget to log nutrition data in my external app?

Open the app before saving your WHOOP Journal to ensure data transfer.

Auto-Log Menstruation

Which apps support Auto-Log Menstruation?

Apple Health; Flo; Cycle Tracking (Apple); Clue

How do I enable Auto-Log Menstrual Cycle Data?

Enable the WHOOP x Apple Health integration and grant WHOOP permission to read menstrual cycle data.

Can I manually add or edit menstrual data in WHOOP?

Yes, you can manually edit menstrual cycle data in the WHOOP app.

General Questions

Do I need to re-enable Apple Health integration if I reinstall the WHOOP app?

Yes, reinstalling WHOOP requires re-enabling the integration in Apple Health.

Why does WHOOP calculate calories differently from Apple Health or other wearables?

WHOOP uses a proprietary algorithm that factors in strain, heart rate, and recovery metrics.

For more details, visit The Science of Calorie Tracking.

Can I delete my WHOOP data from Apple Health?

Yes, you can delete specific data categories or all WHOOP data from Apple Health at any time.

How does Apple Health prioritize data from multiple sources?

Apple Health uses a ranking system to determine which data is displayed. You can adjust priority settings in Apple Health.

Can I control which data WHOOP writes to Apple Health?

Yes, you can toggle individual data categories on or off in WHOOP app settings.

Why doesn’t WHOOP export Recovery activities like Meditation and Stretching to Apple Health?

Currently, Recovery activities such as Meditation and Stretching are not exported to Apple Health.
