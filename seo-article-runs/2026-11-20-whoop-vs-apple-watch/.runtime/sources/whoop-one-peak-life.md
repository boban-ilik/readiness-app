# WHOOP One, Peak and Life tier pages, the free-trial page and the join page (whoop.com)

Publisher: WHOOP (vendor's own pages, /us/en/ locale path).
Captured: 2026-11-20 in the desktop Browser pane (curl returned HTTP 403 from Cloudflare; the pane rendered each page normally, no challenge shown or solved). Text is the pane's deterministic text extraction, verbatim. Nothing was clicked or submitted on the join flow; the cookie banner was left untouched.
What is omitted and why: on the /one/, /peak/ and /life/ pages the "Compare the lineup" block and the "Additional Medical Information" block are word-for-word the same as in `whoop-membership.md` and are not repeated for Peak and Life (they are kept once, in the One capture below). Each page's own text is otherwise complete.
Locale caveat: the session reached the site from outside the United States. The join page showed the line "IHRN is a medically regulated feature and is not available in your region", which is a region-detection message for the visitor, not a statement about the US.
