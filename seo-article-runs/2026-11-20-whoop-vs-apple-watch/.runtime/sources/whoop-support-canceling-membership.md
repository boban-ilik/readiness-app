URL: https://support.whoop.com/s/article/Canceling-Your-Membership?language=en_US
Read: 2026-11-20, in the user's Chrome (page text extracted from the article element). Captured after research.md was completed; evidence ID S1. Relevant passages, verbatim:

- "If you are within the first 30 days of receiving your WHOOP device, you can return it for a refund"
- "Paid upfront (12 months): Schedule your membership to cancel at the end of your commitment period."
- "Paying monthly & within the 12-month commitment: Pay off the remaining months in full or schedule cancellation at the end of the commitment."
- "Paid upfront (24 months): You can schedule your membership to cancel at any time after the initial 30-day return period. Your membership will remain active until the end of your prepaid 24-month term, and the scheduled cancellation will prevent automatic renewal for another 24 months. The commitment period remains 12 months."
- Pause: "Select the duration (up to 3 months)." "While paused: No data will be uploaded. You will not be billed. You can resume at any time." Eligibility: active membership, "Not be in a commitment period", "Not have paused within the last 12 months."
- "If you resume within 90 days, WHOOP will retain your data and avoid a recalibration period. After 90 days, your biometric baseline will be reset, and a recalibration period will be required."
- Free trial: "To avoid being charged at the end of your free trial, you must initiate a return within the 1-month trial period." "A $110 restocking fee applies if the hardware is not returned."
- "What happens after I cancel my membership? Your membership will end after your current commitment period. You can continue using WHOOP and collecting data until the last day of your membership term. Once your membership has ended, WHOOP will no longer collect, upload, or analyze your biometric data."
- "Can I still see my past data after canceling? Yes, you will be able to view your past data, but new data will not be recorded after cancellation."

Limits: this article does not state the 24-month prices, and does not say whether a free trial is offered to every new US buyer today. Those stay Unknown.
