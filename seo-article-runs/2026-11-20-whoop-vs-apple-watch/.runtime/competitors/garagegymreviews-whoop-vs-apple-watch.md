## WHOOP vs Apple Watch: Key Similarities and Differences

Similarities

Both devices monitor key health metrics, including heart rate, calories burned, and blood oxygen levels

Both are meant to be worn around the wrist; though you can wear the WHOOP 5.0 around your biceps or in clothing from the brand’s WHOOP Body line.

You can select from a number of different band color and material options for either device.

Both the WHOOP and the Apple Watch can track more than 100 types of workouts.

Both devices offer sleep and cycle tracking.

Both have vibrating alarms and integrate seamlessly with third-party health and fitness services

Differences

The WHOOP 5.0 is a screenless device; the Apple Watch Series 11 features an OLED always-on retina display.

A WHOOP membership is required to access the device and its insights, with plans ranging from $199 to $359 per year. There is no fee to use the Apple Watch. However, there’s a higher upfront cost for the device itself. 

While the Apple Watch Series 11 has built-in GPS and can record routes without a phone, WHOOP relies on a connected phone for mapped routes.

The WHOOP WHOOPs the Apple Watch in terms of battery life, offering 14 days of usage compared to up to 24 hours of normal use or 38 hours in low-power mode. 

The Apple Watch Series 11 offers superior water resistance (50 meters) than the WHOOP 5.0 (up to 10 meters for two hours). 

Apple Watches allow you to receive and respond to calls, texts, and other notifications from your wrist. WHOOP devices do not.

## Price

It would be unfair to make an apples-to-apples pricing comparison here because WHOOP and Apple market these devices very differently. 

### WHOOP

With WHOOP, you don’t actually pay for the band itself. Instead, you pay a membership fee that allows you to utilize all of its tracking capabilities and features. Here’s a breakdown of the three-tiered system: 

- WHOOP One: Costs $199 per year and includes the WHOOP 5.0 sensor, core recovery, strain, and sleep tracking.
- WHOOP Peak: Costs $239 per year and adds on health and stress monitors, plus longevity data.
- WHOOP Life: Costs $359 per year and includes the medical-grade WHOOP MG sensor, ECG heart screener, and blood pressure insights.

After you’ve completed a 12-month commitment, you can opt for monthly billing. That averages out to $25 per month for WHOOP One, $30 per month for WHOOP Peak, and $40 per month for WHOOP Life.

## Battery Life and Charging

One of the main factors users look for in a fitness tracker is battery life. After all, you want your device to last long enough that you can get a ton done without needing to plug it back in. Who wins the battery life battle between WHOOP vs. Apple Watch? 

### WHOOP

One of the few areas where the WHOOP 5.0 stands head and shoulders above the Apple Watch is battery life. This device can stay powered on for 14 days—a remarkable feat for a fitness tracker. According to WHOOP, it takes about 120 minutes to fully charge the device.

In addition, WHOOP sells a waterproof wireless battery pack for $49 that allows you to fully charge your device as you’re wearing it in about two hours. That’s a super convenient feature that ensures you won’t run out of juice. 

### Apple Watch

The Apple Watch Series 11 has a battery life of up to 24 hours on normal use. According to Apple, it can last for 38 hours on low-power mode. This is solid, but not nearly as impressive as the WHOOP 5.0, which can last for a whopping two weeks. 

GGR expert product tester Kayla Godfrey is a longtime Apple Watch user, but she points out that the battery life is the biggest drawback, even with the Series 11. 

“I have had good luck with the 11 holding its battery, but my last watch would barely make it through the day and die,” she explains. “Typically I can get almost two full days of wearing my watch, but I charge it every night because it is a part of my routine.”

## Fitness and Workout Tracking

### WHOOP

The WHOOP 5.0 gives readings in four critical areas: sleep, recovery, strain, and stress. Of course, you must sync your strap to the WHOOP app to record your workouts—a minor annoyance if you expected your fitness tracker to automatically track everything you do. Thankfully, the app is available for both iOS and Android users.

## Sleep Tracking

Monitoring your heart rate and calorie expenditure can be quite valuable. However, that’s not the only reason to invest in a fitness tracker. Recovery is perhaps the most underappreciated component of a balanced wellness routine, and both the WHOOP 5.0 and Apple Watch Series 11 offer advanced sleep tracking capabilities, too. 

### WHOOP

The WHOOP 5.0 automatically records a number of key sleep metrics, including: 

- Sleep stages: Divides your night into Awake, Light, Deep, and REM sleep
- Sleep performance: How many hours your slept vs. your calculated physiological sleep need
- Sleep efficiency: The percentage of time you spent actually sleeping
- Sleep consistency: How closely your recent bedtimes and wake times align with your circadian rhythm

WHOOP can help you schedule your bed and wake times based on how challenging your day was, when you need to wake up, and what your workout will be the following day. Plus, this sleep data can help you be more aware of your overall health. 

“I can usually predict when I’m getting sick just by watching my sleep, temperature, and recovery numbers,” Sommer explains. “It helps me to up my water, supplements, and sleep time to head off a virus.” 

[image: Sleep analytics in the Whoop app | https://i.garagegymreviews.com/460/870/610/screenshot-from-the-whoop-app-3.webp]

Sleep analytics in the Whoop app

Overall, WHOOP’s biggest advantage is the way it connects sleep to recovery. Overnight heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, and sleep data feed into the daily Recovery score, while the Journal can help identify how behaviors such as alcohol, caffeine, stress, or late meals correlate with sleep and recovery.

“I really like how the health monitoring shows me how alcohol and other factors affect my body,” Sommer says. “This is motivating me to stay on the straight and narrow.”

### Apple Watch

The Apple Watch Series 11 can also provide valuable sleep insights. It estimates total sleep and time spent awake or in Core, Deep, and REM sleep. You can view nightly results or long-term trends in the Sleep and Health apps (another plus for connectivity). 

Your Sleep Score is calculated on a 0-100 scale based on sleep duration, bedtime consistency, and nighttime interruptions. This can give you good context about whether you had a restful night or whether you need to improve your sleep hygiene habits. 

Of course, you don’t have to utilize the Apple Watch for its sleep tracking capabilities. 

“I wanted something that tracked my activity level, but didn’t want to get into the weeds of sleep and recovery like WHOOP,” Kayla explains. “I think Apple does a good job of tracking things like sleep, but I don’t need a device harassing me about things like recovery.” 

## Smartwatch Features

Obviously, WHOOP focuses on activity and sleep tracking, but without a screen, it doesn’t offer as much functionality as the Apple Watch. 

The latter brings connectivity to the forefront, essentially acting as an extension of your iPhone. 

“I like how not only I can track my fitness, but I can also keep all my devices synced together,” Kayla says. “Having my watch allows me to track my activity level, keep up with my important notifications, and also ping my phone when I lose it for the eighth time that day.” 

While I don’t lose my phone that many times on a daily basis, I echo Kayla’s sentiment about the synchronicity factor. It’s quite convenient to be able to quickly check an e-mail or respond to a text message from my wrist without having to pull out my phone. 

Here are some other key smartwatch capabilities you can take advantage of with the Series 11: 

- Safety and location features: Includes Emergency SOS, Fall Detection, Crash Detection, Check in, Find My, and Compass Backtrack for retracing your route. 
- Mobile payments: Leave your credit card at home and use Apple Pay for seamless transactions.
- Notifications: Receive and respond to texts, calls, emails, calendar alerts, and app notifications right from your wrist.

Personally, I love how convenient it is to check into my local gym by just tapping my watch on the scanner or paying for my groceries without pulling out my wallet. While I have a bit of a love-hate relationship with real-time notifications (sometimes it’s nice to disconnect), the Apple Watch has the versatility advantage over the WHOOP 5.0.

### Water Resistance

While the WHOOP 5.0 is “waterproof,” it wasn’t designed for deep sea diving or anything of the like. It only goes up to 10 meters (32 feet), a far cry from the Apple Watch, which can be submerged up to 50 meters (164 feet).

### Cycle Tracking

Women can utilize these devices for cycle support. Here’s an explanation of how each one works: 

#### WHOOP

According to WHOOP, their Menstrual Cycle Insights can: 

- Log cycle phases and correlate with metrics like skin temperature and your Recovery Score
- Track symptoms and display patterns across cycle phases
- Anticipate potential symptoms based on your physiology and past logs
- Predict possible period start, which can help inform your training and recovery
- Overlay phases on trends such as Recovery, Sleep, and Strain metrics
- Provide personalized insights on how hormonal shifts affect your performance, mood, and energy levels

In addition, WHOOP offers Pregnancy Insights that include personalized coaching, symptom tracking, and biometric integration. Plus, they offer a free year-long subscription to Natural Cycles through their new partnership. While you should still work with your healthcare provider, using the device can certainly help you make better-informed decisions.

## Company Policies

From warranties to returns to customer service, these little details can make a huge impact on your purchasing experience. Luckily, both WHOOP and Apple rate highly in this area. 

### WHOOP

WHOOP covers the device against material and workmanship defects for life, as long as you maintain an active membership. In addition, you can try the WHOOP 5.0 for 30 days from the date of delivery before deciding whether you want to return it. You will have to pay for shipping and handling in order to receive a full refund. 

### Apple

Apple isn’t quite so generous, as it only provides a one-year limited warranty. However, you can get added peace of mind and protection with the affordable AppleCare+ program. At just $4.99 per month or $79 for two years for the Series 11 or 12, this protection plan extends your coverage and includes unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection. You can also finance your Apple Watch with an Apple Card, which gives 3% daily cash back upfront on the full cost of your Apple Watch. 

## Which Fitness Tracker Should You Choose?

As a certified personal trainer and lifelong athlete, I’m all about taking advantage of tools that can help you raise your health and fitness game to a new level. That being said, there are distinct differences between these devices that may make one a better choice than the other. 

### Choose WHOOP if:

- You don’t want to be distracted by a screen or phone notifications
- You’re an athlete who wants in-depth sleep and recovery data
- You want maximum battery life for continuous use 

### Choose Apple if:

- You want to remain connected to answer calls and texts
- You’re a runner who needs GPS tracking capabilities
- You want a stylish, sleek smartwatch that can be customized to match your aesthetic

## Final Thoughts

Is WHOOP worth the hype? If you’re less worried about staying in the loop with all your calls, emails, and texts and more focused on getting insight into your health, recovery, and sleep, it’s a solid option. Plus, we like that you aren’t tied down to wearing this gadget around your wrist, as WHOOP’s Body line makes it possible to keep this tracker hidden in a sports bra, leggings, jacket, and other compatible apparel. 

However, the Apple Watch remains one of the best fitness trackers because it checks so many boxes beyond tracking your workouts and sleep. It functions well with a variety of third-party apps and fits seamlessly into the ever-expanding Apple ecosystem. So, if you’re an iPhone user looking to level up your health and fitness in style, it’s hard to go wrong with any of the Apple Watch models on the market. 

Ultimately, you need to decide whether a screenless (aka distraction-free) device that requires a monthly subscription is a better investment than a smartwatch with a higher upfront cost that keeps you connected at all times.
