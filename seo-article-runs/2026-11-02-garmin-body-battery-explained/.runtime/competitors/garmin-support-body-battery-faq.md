The Body Battery energy gauge is a feature available on many Garmin wearables that estimates your energy reserves throughout the day. Using factors such as heart rate variability (HRV), stress, sleep, and activity levels, it provides an energy score ranging from 5 to 100.

To confirm that your device is compatible with this feature, refer to your owner's manual and search for "Body Battery".

For more information, refer to our Garmin Technology page: Health Science - Body Battery Energy Monitoring.

## What Do Charged and Drained Mean?

In your Garmin Connect account, Body Battery includes Charged and Drained values that help explain how your energy levels changed throughout the day.

Charged represents the amount of energy your body recovered.

Drained represents the amount of energy your body used.

Generally, these values should be relatively balanced over a 24-hour period, reflecting a healthy balance between activity and recovery.

The Charged and Drained metrics are calculated over a 24-hour period and reset at midnight. Garmin Connect may also provide personalized insights that highlight Body Battery changes since bedtime or since waking. These insights represent shorter timeframes and may not match the full-day Charged and Drained totals.

## Why Is My Body Battery Low After Sleeping?

How rested you feel and your Body Battery score do not always align. Body Battery considers factors beyond sleep duration, including stress levels and your body's overall recovery.

This is especially noticeable after several consecutive days of consistently high or low Body Battery values. Even if you sleep through the night, elevated stress levels during sleep can reduce how much your Body Battery recharges.

Factors that may increase stress levels include:

- Bright lights
- Alcohol consumption
- Excessive physical activity
- Physical exhaustion
- Extreme temperatures
- Loud noises
- Pain or discomfort
- Illness
- Frequent waking, tossing, or turning during sleep

## Why Is My Body Battery Stuck at 5?

### New Device or Recently Reset Device

Body Battery requires a learning period to establish your personal baseline and provide accurate readings. If you recently started using a new Garmin device or performed a factory reset, this learning period typically lasts 5-7 days.

During this time, your Body Battery may remain at or near 5 while enough data is collected to establish your baseline. Once the learning period is complete, Body Battery should begin functioning normally.

### Recalibration

In some cases, your device may need to recalibrate Body Battery and all-day stress tracking. If this baseline is lost, Body Battery may drain to 5 and remain there until enough physiological data is collected to reestablish it.

This recalibration process happens automatically and can take several days.

## What Happens If I Do Not Wear My Device?

Body Battery can estimate charging and draining during short periods when the device is not worn. However, if the device is removed for several hours or longer, Body Battery will resume from a model-based estimate when it is worn again.

For the most accurate results, wear your device as consistently as possible throughout the day and night, and ensure it fits properly for reliable sensor readings.

## What If I Use Another Garmin Device That Does Not Have Body Battery?

For the most accurate Body Battery tracking, wear your Body Battery-compatible device while using another Garmin device.

Data from devices that do not support Body Battery will not be included in Body Battery calculations.

## Which Models Keep Body Battery Data in Sync?

Body Battery TrueUp keeps Body Battery data in sync across multiple Garmin products. For example, the Index Sleep Monitor syncs sleep data with Garmin Connect, which then shares this data with the device. TrueUp utilizes this information to maintain accurate Body Battery readings.

You can still use a non-listed Garmin device to update Body Battery in Garmin Connect, and that data will sync with any compatible devices you own. However, Body Battery data from a compatible device will not transfer to a non-compatible device.
