# Apple Watch models

Welcome to Apple Watch, the versatile wrist-worn companion that can help motivate you to be more active, track important health information, stay in touch with those who matter most to you, and so much more—with or without your iPhone.

This guide helps you discover all the amazing things Apple Watch can do with watchOS 27, which is compatible with the following models:

- [Apple Watch Series 12](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#84aef9a8-cacd-41fd-bf49-b2ae39a0bf40)
- [Apple Watch Ultra 4](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#4a85789b-e0ca-4b27-b509-7fa1d7ccff05)
- [Apple Watch Series 11](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#apd4fa68f56f)
- [Apple Watch Ultra 3](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#apd2cb142009)
- [Apple Watch SE 3](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#apde98a33e0a)
- [Apple Watch Series 10](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#apd87a980dfb)
- [Apple Watch Ultra 2](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/applewatch-models-apd2054d0d5b/27/watchos/27#apda9a7ae6e7)

## Apple Watch Series 12

![The front of Apple Watch Series 12, with the display showing the watch face, and the Digital Crown, microphone, and side button from top to bottom on the side of the watch.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/8ed5ec4b-a9bb-4fde-8d64-be0e49cd89af)
![The back of Apple Watch Series 12, with the band release buttons at top and bottom, the health sensing system in the middle, and the speaker on the side.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/ed589eb8-f98c-491e-aa6e-38217399b7c9)
*Note:* Blood Oxygen app measurements are not intended for medical use. For models of Apple Watch purchased in the United States on or after January 18, 2024 with part numbers ending in LW/A or LS/A, the Blood Oxygen data analysis is performed on iPhone, and results can be viewed in the Health app. Learn how to [identify your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/108056).
