Body Insights is chronic illness management for people who crash.
It is a symptom tracker for pacing with ME/CFS, long COVID, POTS or fatigue.
See your energy budget on a bad day or a flare, and get a hint to rest.

PACE A CHRONIC ILLNESS (Premium)
Body Insights reads and interprets Apple Health data so you can plan each day around the energy you have.
• Not sure how much to do today? Coach gives daily guidance that respects your limits.
• Wake up drained? Your daily state shows what kind of day this is before you make plans.
• Too tired to open the app? Log a check-in in seconds from iPhone or Apple Watch. Record a day score, symptom tags, and a note. Tags include fatigue, brain fog, aches, unrefreshing sleep, dizzy on standing, headache, heart racing and more, plus custom tags.
• Running low by midday? Your energy budget shows the energy you can expect today, what you have spent, and what is left.
• Bad days seem random? Patterns show what tends to follow your busier days, so you can plan rest.
• Symptoms scattered across notes? Common check-in symptoms, such as fatigue, headache and dizziness, are saved to Apple Health.
• Appointment coming up? Share a doctor report: a PDF of your last 30 or 90 days.
• Your personal step ceiling adds one more signal to your daily guidance.

WHEN YOUR SIGNALS SHIFT (Premium)
Body Insights compares you to your own baseline, not to an average person.
• Symptom Radar alerts you when your signals move away from your usual levels, a hint to rest.

UNDERSTAND EVERY DAY (Free)
Body Insights turns Apple Watch data into plain daily scores for people who want to understand their body.
• Body Battery: see your energy across the day. The energy forecast, circadian view and alerts are Premium.
• Sleep score: understand how last night went.
• Daily stress: see your stress load. Resilience and Burnout views are Premium.
• Morning Report: a short summary to start your day, free 3 per week, unlimited with Premium.
• Fitness Age: your cardio fitness shown as an age.
• Biological Age: add your lab results to see an age profile from your blood work.
• Home Screen widgets for Body Battery and stress.

WANT MORE DETAIL? (Premium)
Body Insights shows how your heart, nervous system and sleep compare to your age.
• Heart Age, Nervous System Age, Sleep Age and Pace of Aging.
• CGM glucose next to your sleep and workouts, read from Apple Health.
• DNA Analysis: add a raw DNA file for supplement, nutrition and medication gene insights.
• On Apple Watch, start a heart rate alert session. You get a tap on the wrist when your heart rate crosses limits you set.
• Training load to balance training and rest.
• More Home Screen widgets, such as Resilience and Coach.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Free: Body Battery, sleep score, daily stress, Fitness Age, Biological Age, Body Battery and stress widgets and 3 Morning Reports per week.
Premium adds: the chronic illness module, Symptom Radar, Coach, doctor report, Heart, Nervous System and Sleep Age, CGM glucose, DNA Analysis, training load, the Apple Watch app, more widgets and unlimited Morning Reports.

Version History HTML_TAG_START Your Apple Watch collects a lot of health data. Body Insights turns it into plain advice you can use.

Most health apps show numbers. Body Insights explains what those numbers may mean for your day. It reads sleep, heart rate, HRV, temperature, stress, workouts, recovery, cycle context, and energy. Then it gives you a simple daily picture: push, take it easy, or recover.

You do not need a new wearable. If you already use Apple Watch and Apple Health, Body Insights helps you understand the data you already have. The Apple Watch experience gives you quick reads from your wrist, with clearer states when data has not synced yet.

FOR WOMEN

Body Insights can personalize sleep, exercise guidance, and Symptom Radar around cycle context. Temperature changes can mean different things at different points in the cycle. Body Insights uses that context before treating a change as unusual.

QUESTIONS BODY INSIGHTS HELPS ANSWER

Can I push today?
Body Insights looks at recovery, sleep, stress, heart rate, cycle context, and recent activity. It helps you choose a harder day or a lighter day.

Why do I feel tired?
Body Insights checks Body Battery, sleep debt, stress load, HRV, and recovery trends so you can see what may be draining you.

Are my body signals changing?
Symptom Radar watches temperature, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, recovery, and cycle context. It does not diagnose anything. It helps you notice when your body is acting differently than usual.

FEATURES

- Apple Watch: quick health and recovery signals from your wrist
- Body Battery: a simple estimate of usable energy today
- Waking Recovery: a clear read on whether your body is ready for more
- Sleep Score and Sleep Debt: see sleep quality and missed sleep
- HRV and RMSSD: plain-language stress and recovery signals
- Training Load: see whether recent workouts match recovery
- Resilience Score: understand how much strain your body may handle today
- Symptom Radar: watch temperature, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, recovery, and cycle context
- Burnout Manager: see when stress and low recovery are stacking up
- Fitness Age: understand your fitness level in a simple way
- Glucose Insights: connect compatible CGM data with sleep, stress, workouts, and recovery

WHO BODY INSIGHTS IS FOR

Body Insights is for people who want to understand their body without reading charts all day. It is useful if you train, recover slowly, travel often, manage stress, or want guidance that can account for women's cycle context.
