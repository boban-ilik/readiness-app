## What is Body Insights?

Body Insights is a chronic illness app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is for people who pace ME/CFS, Long COVID or POTS, and who crash after a day that looked fine. It reads the Apple Health data your watch already saved: HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, steps, wrist temperature and breathing rate. On a day below your usual, rest stays the plan.

- **Symptom Radar** sends a notification when readings sit off your usual.
- **Daily state** puts today next to your own usual days, including a resting heart rate that sits high.
- **Check-ins** keep the harder day after the busier day, in your words: fatigue, brain fog, worse after activity.
- **The doctor report** is a PDF of those days for the visit.

The download is free. No account. No new band.
