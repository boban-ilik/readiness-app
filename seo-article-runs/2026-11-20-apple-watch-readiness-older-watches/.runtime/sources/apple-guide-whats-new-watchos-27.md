# What’s new in watchOS 27

## Siri AI (Beta)

**Introducing Siri AI (Beta).** Siri AI is powered by Apple Intelligence* and makes Siri more helpful than ever. Siri AI is your conversational AI assistant that draws on broad world knowledge, personal context, and what’s on your Apple Watch to get relevant answers and find what you need. Siri AI can also take action in apps using your personal context to help you get things done. And explore the new Siri app to view and continue previous conversations, or start a new one. See [What’s new with Siri AI](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/get-started-with-siri-ai-gg3z5mqk2u1k/27/watchos/27) and [Use the Siri app](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/use-the-siri-app-wzgenr1pwbwv/27/watchos/27).

![The Siri app showing two conversations. A button to start a new conversation appears at the bottom of the screen.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/dc2f544b-1d44-4bee-821e-0f4cf1c0d6e4)
*Note:* Siri AI is available in beta on watchOS 27 or later on Apple Watch Series 9 and later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 and later, and Apple Watch SE 3 when paired with an Apple Intelligence–enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later with Siri and device language set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not initially be available in the EU on iOS or watchOS. Apple Intelligence and Siri AI are not available in China mainland. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Usage limits may apply.

## Apple Intelligence

**Workout Buddy enhancements.**You can get new data insights with Workout Buddy, including your progress for pace, distance, and workout duration. You can also use Workout Buddy without your paired iPhone nearby with a Wi-Fi or cellular connection. See [Use Workout Buddy](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/use-workout-buddy-apd65c7938e6/27/watchos/27).

*Note:* Available in English and Spanish.

**Get relevant information while on a call.**With Call Context, you can proactively surface relevant information from your apps when you’re calling a business—like a confirmation code from Mail when you call an airline. See [While on a call](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/while-on-a-call-apd444b1e721/27/watchos/27).

![The call screen, showing a Call Context card with a flight confirmation.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/a4ec4ae6-cb5e-4875-8228-598f9dadb49e)

## Health and fitness

**Readiness.**On Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, use the new Readiness app to view a score that helps you understand when you’re likely ready to push yourself and when it’s time to take a rest. See [Use the Readiness app on Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/readiness-flx4gnzby346/27/watchos/27).

![The Readiness app showing a readiness score.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/de649d1c-841a-48e2-948b-c82dd8aaea6e)
**Heart Rate.**On Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, you can view a chart of your heart rate measurements as it updates in real time. You can also view your heart rate variability (HRV), which measures the overall variation in time between heart beats. See [See your heart rate](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/heart-rate-apda88aefe4c/27/watchos/27#apdda0d3f1a2) and [View your recovery heart rate variability](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/heart-rate-apda88aefe4c/27/watchos/27#ec659939-60e9-42b2-8e7d-86762dc6461f).

![The Heart Rate app showing a heart rate reading. A graph of heart rate data appears at the bottom of the screen.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/22938a04-dd0e-439a-933e-5ec03dc45e0c)
**Vitals.**On Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, you can see additional health metrics collected during the day—resting heart rate and heart rate variability. See [View your daytime vitals](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/vitals-apd15aa7ed96/27/watchos/27#79e4c890-625a-4126-8fc8-9d747680267f).

![A screen in the Vitals app showing daytime health metrics.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/18c2e592-faa8-4504-9a47-1d32feefd65d)
**Cycle Tracking.**Cycle Tracking in the Health app on iPhone supports perimenopause and menopause. You can log your symptoms, and find support with educational resources. If you’re 40 and older, you can receive a notification when your logged cycle patterns are suggestive of perimenopause or other underlying conditions. See [Track menopause stages](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/track-menopause-stages-pym0x6ny1te5/27/watchos/27).

**Activity.**The redesigned pedometer model uses new machine learning algorithms to help measure the distance of your treadmill runs and walks more precisely. And on Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, the redesigned pedometer model and S11 chip are used to also provide more accurate step counts all day. You can also use the new Steps complication on Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 to track your steps in real time, right from your watch face. See [Start a workout](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/start-a-workout-apdd16e8761a/27/watchos/27) and [Track daily activity](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/track-daily-activity-apd3bf6d85a6/27/watchos/27).

**Workout.**Extended Workout mode turns on Low Power Mode for the duration of your workout and certain running metrics are unavailable. For even longer battery life, you can also start a Max Extended Workout for Outdoor Walk, Outdoor Run, and Hiking workouts. Certain metrics and splits are unavailable during a Max Extended Workout. See [Conserve power during workouts](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/start-a-workout-apdd16e8761a/27/watchos/27#239b471f-dc67-429a-ad18-1b835f97310f).

## Convenience and productivity

**Find My app.**Find Devices, Find People, and Find Items are now consolidated into the single Find My app. Everything you care about is easier to locate on a map-centric view. See [Set up Find My](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/set-up-find-my-jg49jxgqs19v/27/watchos/27).

![A Find My screen showing a friend's location on a map.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/fd2b4907-67b9-48e5-aede-6153e609dc16)
**Open Smart Stack widgets with a single tap.**When you’re viewing widgets in the Smart Stack, perform the single tap gesture to select a widget. See [Use tap gestures](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/use-tap-gestures-apdabb7b275c/27/watchos/27).

**Discover music in an instant.**On Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, Shazam leverages Apple’s on-device audio intelligence to quickly help identify music in the Smart Stack. See [Identify songs with Shazam](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/music-recognition-apdff3d90679/27/watchos/27).

**Sound Recognition in Accessibility.**Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 can continuously listen for certain sounds—such as a doorbell, siren, or crying baby—and notify you when it recognizes these sounds. See [Recognize sounds using Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/use-sound-recognition-ay39m4y6riek/27/watchos/27).

## Watch faces and personalization

**Dynamic app grid.**Get to your apps faster with the new dynamic app grid. This automatically highlights Siri-suggested apps, including your most popular and recently used apps—with the Siri app always in the center. See [Use apps on Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/use-apps-apda1bf1a95b/27/watchos/27).

**Explore new watch faces.**Switch things up with new watch faces for Apple Watch. Pride Luminance is a dynamic analog face that is inspired by multiple Pride flags. And use the Siri Modular face for making quick requests to Siri. See [Apple Watch faces and their features](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/faces-and-features-apde9218b440/27/watchos/27).

![The Siri Modular watch face. The date appears at the top of the screen, followed by the time. Workout, Siri, and Music complications appear at the bottom of the screen.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/3a603884-2fd8-4189-804d-f9ca5b519429)
