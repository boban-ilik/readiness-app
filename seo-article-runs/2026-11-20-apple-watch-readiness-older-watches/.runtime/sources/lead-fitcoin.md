# Does Apple Watch Have a Readiness Score? (2026: Yes, on Series 12 and Ultra 4)

Published August 14, 2026 • Updated September 10, 2026 • By [Harris Khan](/about#harris-khan) • 12 min read

![Fitcoin fitness rewards app on iPhone and Android, with Sleep, Recovery and Energy](/images/og/fitcoin-og-f-800.webp)

**Update, September 10, 2026: Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 now have a native Readiness score.** Everything below still applies to every other Apple Watch, including Series 9, 10 and 11 on watchOS 27, which do not get it. For the new feature itself, read [Apple Watch Readiness Score: Series 12, Ultra 4 and how it compares](/articles/apple-watch-series-12-readiness-score). If you are on Series 11 or earlier and just want the score, start with [how to get readiness on an older Apple Watch](/articles/how-to-get-readiness-older-apple-watch).

**Until this week, Apple Watch did not have a Readiness Score.** Oura does. Whoop has Recovery with colours. Garmin has Body Battery, which is a tank, not a green light. Apple ships measurements and a Sleep Score, then lets the App Store invent the coaching number. That is a design habit, not a missing toggle in Settings.

If you want the energy-tank comparison, see [Body Battery](/articles/apple-watch-body-battery). If you want Whoop’s colours, see [Whoop Recovery explained](/articles/whoop-recovery-score-explained). If you want the alternatives list, see [recovery apps without Whoop](/articles/apple-watch-recovery-apps-without-whoop). If you already own a ring, start with [Oura vs Apple Watch](/articles/oura-vs-apple-watch).

**Short verdict:** On Series 12 and Ultra 4 there is now a Readiness app, 0 to 10, tied to the new Health Sensing System. On every older watch there is no hidden Readiness tile in watchOS. Use Sleep Score for last night’s behaviour. Use a Watch Recovery app, or how you feel, for whether to push or go easy. Apple 82 and Athlytic 61 are two recipes, not a pair you average.

## What watchOS actually ships

- **Sleep Score** is 0–100: about half duration, 30 points schedule, 20 interruptions. Labels run from Very Low to Very High on current iOS / watchOS 26.2 copy.
- **Sleep stages** still show in the Sleep app. They do not own the 100.
- **HRV** is stored as SDNN samples in Health, not a morning RMSSD ritual.
- **Resting heart rate** is a daily sample that can move after you wake.
- **Wrist temperature** on Series 8+ is a deviation from your baseline, not a thermometer.
- **Training Load / effort views** on current watchOS cover recent strain, not a readiness stamp.

None of that is Readiness 74, go easy. Apple’s personality is to show the measurement. Oura’s personality is to collapse it. Third-party Watch apps exist because a lot of people want the second personality without a ring or a strap.

## Readiness, Recovery, Energy: three different jobs

| Word people use | Job | Native on Apple Watch? |
| Readiness (Oura) | Morning: can I absorb strain? | No |
| Recovery (Whoop / Bevel / Athlytic / Fitcoin) | Morning: push or easy? | No native headline. Apps yes. |
| Sleep Score (Apple) | Last night's behaviour | Yes, watchOS 26 |
| Body Battery (Garmin) | Same-day energy tank | No |
| Energy (Fitcoin / Bevel Energy Bank) | What is left / rolling account | No native tank |

If an App Store screenshot says Readiness and the app is reading Apple Health, it is usually a Recovery score with friendlier marketing. Fine. Just know Apple did not bless the integer.

## What actually fills the gap

**Athlytic** uses sleeping HRV and resting heart rate versus a 60-day baseline, plus a Target Exertion Zone on the wrist. See the [Athlytic review](/articles/athlytic-review).

**Bevel** Recovery is 1–100% from a longer ingredient list, plus Strain and Energy Bank. For the glossary, see [Bevel scores explained](/articles/bevel-scores-explained). For the head-to-head, see [Bevel vs Athlytic](/articles/bevel-vs-athlytic).

**Fitcoin Recovery** is the morning autonomic picture. A separate Energy tank covers the afternoon. SleepScore covers the night. Then FitScore covers the session you still did. Disclosed.

**Oura or Whoop:** if you wanted the original named score, that is the original hardware. Watch apps cannot promise the ring’s Readiness or Whoop’s colours. For the hardware comparison, see [Oura vs Apple Watch](/articles/oura-vs-apple-watch) and [Fitcoin vs Whoop](/articles/fitcoin-vs-whoop).

## Vitals is not Readiness either

Apple's Vitals-style views (overnight heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, sleep duration versus your norms) are deviation dashboards. Useful when two signals jump together. They still will not print a coaching percentage. Immune-style products, including ours, use those deviations as a cap, not as a diagnosis. If you feel ill, a human beats an app.

Training Load going up while Sleep Score is High and HRV looks rude is the interesting Tuesday. That is the closest Apple gets to a readiness conversation without naming it. You still have to do the reading.

## Why Apple skipped a branded Readiness

Same reason there is no Body Battery: Apple likes the measurement in Health and dislikes looking like a coach that can be sued. A 0-100 that says 'you may train' is a product and a liability. Third parties can eat that. Apple sells watches.

That is also why Sleep Score weights behaviour you can see (hours, bedtime, wake-ups) instead of REM politics. Readiness wants HRV. HRV is noisy. Apple would rather you open Health than argue with a colour.

## A few easy mix-ups

- One Recovery headline is easier to live with than four apps averaged together.
- Sleep Score 96 is a good night, not a licence for doubles.
- If the Watch was off overnight, there will not be a Readiness tile waiting in Settings. Apple never shipped one.
- A ring is worth buying for comfort and membership, not because a thread said Apple is ‘behind’.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Apple Watch have a Readiness Score?

It depends on the watch. [Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4](/articles/apple-watch-series-12-readiness-score), announced September 9, 2026, ship a native Readiness score from 0 to 10 with a Recover, Pace Yourself, Ready or Go For It recommendation. Every earlier model, including Series 9, 10 and 11 on watchOS 27, has no native readiness number: you get Sleep Score, HRV samples, resting heart rate, wrist temperature and Training Load, and third-party apps supply the Recovery verdict.

### Is Apple Sleep Score the same as Readiness?

No. Sleep Score is last night’s behaviour: duration, schedule, interruptions. Readiness is whether the autonomic picture looks ready to absorb strain today. You can sleep High and still be a bad idea for intervals.

### What is the closest Apple Watch readiness app?

Athlytic Recovery plus Target Exertion, Bevel Recovery, Gentler Streak, or Fitcoin Recovery. They will not match Oura Readiness. Pick one headline.

### Does Garmin Body Battery count as readiness?

Body Battery is a same-day energy tank, not a morning readiness stamp. That is a different job. See [Does Apple Watch have Body Battery?](/articles/apple-watch-body-battery)

### Is Training Load a readiness score?

No. Training Load is about recent effort. Readiness is about whether you should add more. Useful together. Not the same integer.
